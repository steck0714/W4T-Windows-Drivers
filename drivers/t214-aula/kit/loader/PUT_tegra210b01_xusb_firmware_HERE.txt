USB on Windows 11 needs NVIDIA's XUSB firmware for Tegra X1+ (T210B01), which cannot be
redistributed here. Take lib/firmware/tegra210b01_xusb_firmware (122368 bytes) from the
initramfs of a Switchroot Ubuntu image and copy it to the root of this FAT partition, next to
this file. Without it Windows works as before, just without USB. See the README, section USB.
